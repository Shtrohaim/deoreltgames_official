3.2 AppMetrica (part of the advertising SDK)

Together with Yandex Mobile Ads, the app includes the AppMetrica library by Yandex and an ad revenue adapter. The advertising SDK uses it to send Yandex technical data about ad impressions and ad revenue.

Data that may be processed by AppMetrica:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system version, language and region;
  • app version;
  • ad impression events and ad revenue information;
  • SDK diagnostic and crash data.

This data is used to count ad impressions and revenue, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and keep the SDK stable. The developer does not use AppMetrica to track your in-game actions and does not send game data to it.
